The Impact of Counting Mistakes: Error -7 to +7

What happens if you miss a card, drop the count, or miscalculate? Here is the exact mathematical damage to your Hourly EV, Volatility, and Edge when your count is systematically off by -7 to +7 points.

🛡️
1. The Forgiving Corridor (±1 to ±2)
You do not have to be a computer! If your count is off by ±1 or ±2 points, your net profit stays between $21.49 and $26.16/hr (within 10% of optimal). Human imperfection does not ruin your advantage.
💣
2. Negative Error (-k): Over-Betting Danger
If your count is 3 to 6 points lower than reality (e.g. real RC is 16, but you think it's 12), you trigger too early. You place $25 bets on weak shoes, causing your standard deviation to surge by +44% (up to $647/hr). At -7, EV goes negative (-$5.66/hr).
⏳
3. Positive Error (+k): Starvation Danger
If your count is 3 to 6 points higher than reality (e.g. real RC is 12, but you think it's 15), you trigger too late. You sit like a sitting duck paying -$26/hr on main bets without ever firing the side bet. At +7, EV goes negative (-$4.01/hr).
⚖️
4. Which Mistake is Worse?
Under-counting (-k) is more dangerous to your wallet: it triggers massive bankroll drawdowns and requires an enormous stop-loss. Over-counting (+k) is boring and starves EV, but keeps volatility low ($237/hr). When in doubt, round up (higher count)!
