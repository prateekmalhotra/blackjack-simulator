The Undisputed Sweet Spot: Trigger at RC ≤ 12 (or Count ≤ -12)

Selecting a trigger is a classic Goldilocks optimization problem. Triggering too high (RC 15–16) suffers from The Dilution Trap (spiking variance for negligible gain). Triggering too low (RC 8–9) suffers from The Starvation Trap (bleeding money on main bets while waiting). RC ≤ 12 delivers the highest Risk-Adjusted Return (Sharpe / SCORE), capturing +11.8% edge on 18.3% of hands with minimal downside.

⚠️
1. The Dilution Trap (RC ≥ 15)
At RC 16 (Count -8), you trigger on 34.7% of hands, but your side bet edge is diluted to just +6.14%. Your standard deviation explodes to $585/hr—a 31% surge in brutal bankroll swings for essentially zero additional net hourly profit!
⏳
2. The Starvation Trap (RC ≤ 8)
At RC 8 (Count -16), your side bet edge is a massive +20.1%! But it only happens on 7.89% of rounds. While waiting, you bleed -$26/hr flat-betting negative-EV main bets. Your net hourly profit collapses by 52% down to just $13.52/hr!
🎯
3. The Sweet Spot Math (RC 12)
Total EV is the product of Frequency × Edge. At RC 12, you capture 18.3% of hands at an outstanding +11.8% edge. This produces the optimal peak where your side bet earnings ($54/hr) dwarf main bet bleed while keeping variance comfortably low ($446/hr).
⚖️
4. Risk-Adjusted SCORE Peak
In professional advantage play, SCORE = (EV / SD)² measures how fast your bankroll grows relative to ruin risk. The SCORE curve peaks sharply between RC 11 and RC 13. RC 12 delivers the highest confidence, fastest doubling time, and lowest required stop-loss.
