\[ \mathbb{E}[X] = \sum_{i=0}^{7} P(\text{lammers} = i) \times \text{Payout}(i) \] \[ \mathbb{E}[X] = (0.8166 \times -1) + (0.1599 \times 3) + (0.0167 \times 12) + (0.0052 \times 30) + (0.0011 \times 50) + (0.0005 \times 100) \] \[ \mathbb{E}[X] = -0.8166 + 0.4797 + 0.2004 + 0.1560 + 0.0550 + 0.0500 = \mathbf{+0.1091 \text{ units (+10.91%)}} \]

\[ \text{Net EV per Hand} = \$25 \times (+0.1091) = \mathbf{+\$2.73 \text{ per trigger round}} \]

4. Why the 1-Lammer Outcome Alone Loses Money

Notice what happens if we isolate just the 1-lammer outcome:

  • Cost of missed hands: \( 0.8166 \times \$25 = \$20.42 \) lost per hand.
  • Revenue from 1 lammer: \( 0.1599 \times \$75 = \$11.99 \) won per hand.
  • Net without multi-lammer cascades: \( \$11.99 - \$20.42 = -\$8.43 \) (A -33.7% loss!).

This proves your observation: Single lammers keep you in the fight, but 2-lammer ($300), 3-lammer ($750), and 4+ lammer ($1,250+) cascades generate 100% of the net profit!
