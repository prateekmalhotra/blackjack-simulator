⚠️ The Count -11 Trap: Why We Don't Bet $10 Here

At Count -11, the side bet edge is only +0.16% (PT1) to +1.04% (PT2). On a $10 bet, that earns just 1.6 cents. Meanwhile, your $10 main bet loses -10.4¢ to the house in 3:2 and -24.7¢ in 6:5. Adding a side bet at -11 leaves you in the red while risking $10 on an 82% losing proposition. Discipline rule: Wait for ≤ -12!
