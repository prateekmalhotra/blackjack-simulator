⚖️ Why EV / SD is the King of Advantage Play
1. The Airplane Analogy (Speed vs Turbulence)

EV is your flying speed (how fast your bankroll travels). SD is the atmospheric turbulence (the size of your swings). Flying at 100 mph in smooth air gets you to your destination reliably; flying at 100 mph in a Category 5 hurricane risks crashing the plane.

Triggering at RC ≤ 12 delivers the maximum EV-to-SD efficiency ($0.0627$), getting you to safety in 254 hands ($N_0$), compared to 466 hands at RC 16 or 562 hands at RC 8!

2. Risk of Ruin & Stop-Loss Sizing

Because Risk of Ruin scales with $e^{-2 \cdot \text{Bankroll} \cdot (\text{EV}/\text{SD}^2)}$, a strategy with high $\text{EV}/\text{SD}$ cuts your required bankroll in half.
