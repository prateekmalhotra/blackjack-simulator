🏺 Pot of Gold: Hit Distribution by POG2 Count

Empirical distribution across 300,000 simulated rounds per table format (6 Decks, 83% Pen, PT2 Paytable).

🔑 Key Distribution Takeaway:
Notice the dramatic spike in Pot of Gold hit rate as POG2 Running Count drops below ≤ 12 (highlighted in gold). At neutral counts (RC ~24), hit frequency hovers around ~14-16%. As the count dives into single digits and negative territory (deep small card density), the hit rate climbs up to 25% – 35%+ per round, while enabling multi-lammer cascades (2, 3, 4+ lammers paying 12:1, 30:1, 50:1, 100:1)!
