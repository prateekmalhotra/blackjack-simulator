72: ## 5. The D10 Break-Even vs. D12 Staking Trigger
75: * **D10 = Down 10** ($\text{RC} = 14$, or Count $-10$ from zero).
76: * **D12 = Down 12** ($\text{RC} = 12$, or Count $-12$ from zero).
78: ### Why D10 is the Break-Even Line:
79: * At a fresh shuffle (Count $0$ / $\text{RC} = 24$), the Pot of Gold carries a massive **$-13.53\%$ house edge**.
80: * As 10s and Aces exit, that house advantage steadily erodes.
81: * **At D10 (Count $-10$ / $\text{RC} = 14$): The house edge hits $\approx 0.00\%$!**
82: * With Fives Farming active, the player edge is **$-0.57\%$ to $+0.47\%$** (virtually a dead-even coin flip, losing only ~$\approx \$0.14$ to $\$0.25$ per $\$25$ bet).
83: * At **D11 (Count $-11$ / $\text{RC} = 13$):** The edge crosses into positive territory (**$+0.43\%$ to $+1.63\%$**).
85: ### Why the Playbook Triggers at D12 instead of D10:
86: If the house edge disappears at D10, why does Rule 2 demand waiting until **D12 ($\text{RC} \le 12$)**?
87: 1. **Mandatory Main Bet Drag:** On your own spot, table rules require a Main Bet. On 6:5 Free Bet BJ, the main bet carries a **$-1.5\%$ to $-1.8\%$ house edge** ($-\$0.15$ to $-\$0.18$ per $\$10$ bet). At D10, a $0.0\%$ side bet does not overcome this table tax—your round EV is still negative. At **D12**, the side bet generates **$+4.11\%$ EV** ($+\$1.03$ on $\$25$), easily overcoming the main bet loss to yield a net session profit of **+$0.88/hand**.
88: 2. **Variance Compensation:** Pot of Gold has an 82% loss rate and 18% hit rate. APs never take on heavy side bet variance for a $0\%$ edge; you demand at least **$+3\%$ to $+4\%$ buffer** before risking bankroll capital.
89: 3. **The "Friendly Spot" Exception:** If you use the stealth tactic of placing $25 on an adjacent friendly player's side bet spot (where **they pay the main bet** and you have **zero main bet drag**), D10 is an exact 50/50 break-even game, and D11/D12 delivers pure un-taxed $+EV$!
