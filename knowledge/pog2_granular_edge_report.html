🎯 Why the "10% at -12" Rule of Thumb Exists

The Crossover vs The Pool Average

You remembered "10% edge at -12" because $-12$ is your entry gate to a pool of rounds that averages +10.45%!

When a shoe reaches Count $-12$, it rarely stays parked exactly at $-12$. As more cards are dealt:

  • 32% of the time: The shoe hovers around $-12$ to $-13$ (where your edge is +4.1%).
  • 41% of the time: The shoe dives deeper into $-14$ to $-17$ (where your edge jumps to +8% to +14%).
  • 27% of the time: The shoe hits a super-rich vein of small cards and plunges past $-18$ to $-24$ (where your edge explodes to +16% to +33.5%).

When you weight every single triggered round by its real shoe frequency, your average return on capital is +10.45%! That means over an extended session:

  • Every time you bet 1 spot of $40 Side Bet: You are realizing +$4.18 in pure mathematical EV.
  • Every time you bet 2 spots of $40 Side Bet ($80 total): You are realizing +$8.36 in pure mathematical EV per round.

💎 Fives Farming: The Turbocharger of Your Edge

On standard Free Bet Blackjack, basic strategy dictates that a player should double on a pair of 5,5 (hard 10).

The POG Advantage Play Deviation

When the Pot of Gold side bet is active ($\text{Count} \le -12$), you must SPLIT 5s instead of doubling.

Why? Splitting 5,5 accomplishes three massive things:

  1. Instant Free Lammer: The split itself is free and awards an automatic lammer.
  2. Secondary Double Opportunities: Each 5 has an enormous probability of catching a 4, 5, or 6, giving you another Free Double on 9, 10, or 11 on the split hands!
  3. Lammer Stacking: A single hand starting with 5,5 frequently produces 2, 3, or even 4 lammers, paying $12:1$ ($480) or $30:1$ ($1,200).
