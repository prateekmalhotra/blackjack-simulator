The Small-Card Engine
As the count plunges to Count ≤ -12 (RC ≤ 12), the shoe is rich with 2s, 3s, 4s, 5s, 6s, and 7s. This drives a double surge: pairs of small cards trigger Free Splits, while totals of 5 or 6 readily draw 4s, 5s, or 6s to trigger Free Doubles!
⚡
Multi-Lammer Cascades
While a 1-lammer hit pays 3:1, multi-lammer hands pay 12:1, 30:1, and 50:1+. In deep negative territory, hands producing 2, 3, or 4+ lammers surge by +50% to +110% compared to neutral counts, driving exponential EV growth.
🛡️
Dealer Blackjack Shield
If the dealer gets a natural 21, the side bet is completely forfeited ($0 payout). At high positive counts, dealer BJ strikes 6.13% of the time. But in deep trigger counts, the lack of 10s and Aces cuts dealer BJ frequency in half to 2.97%!
📈
The Break-Even Threshold
At neutral (Count 0 / RC 24), the house enjoys a -10.1% edge. The player crosses into positive territory at Count ≤ -10 (RC ≤ 14), accelerating to a massive +38.3% player edge at extreme counts!
